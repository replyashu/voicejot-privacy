VoiceJot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

VoiceJot is designed to be private by default.

Summary: VoiceJot has no account. Your notes stay on your device. We collect nothing and show no ads. The app goes online for one optional thing only: if you switch on AI pictures for a presentation, that presentation's slide titles (shown to you first) are sent to draw them. Leave it off and nothing is ever sent.

What we collect

Nothing. VoiceJot has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Your notes

Your notes are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. VoiceJot never sends your notes anywhere, and there is no VoiceJot server that stores anything about you. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Android offers apps an automatic backup to the user's own Google Drive. VoiceJot excludes your notes from it, so they are not copied to any cloud service, including Google's. On Android 12 and newer they are still included when you transfer directly from an old phone to a new one, because that copies straight between the two devices; on older versions of Android the two cannot be separated, so notes are left out of that transfer as well. Either way, Settings › Backup exports everything as a file you control.

Locked folders

A folder can be locked. Its notes are then encrypted on the device with a key held in the Android Keystore and released only after you confirm your fingerprint, PIN, pattern or password. The key never leaves the device's secure hardware, and VoiceJot keeps the decrypted notes in memory only while the folder is open — closing the app locks it again.

Locked folders need Android 11 or newer. On older versions the system cannot tie an encryption key to your PIN or pattern — only to a fingerprint — which would mean losing every note in the folder the moment a new fingerprint was added to the phone. Rather than ship that, the feature is simply absent there.

Two things this does not cover, stated plainly: the folder's name is stored unencrypted so the app can show that it exists while locked, so avoid putting anything revealing in the name; and a locked note cannot carry a reminder, because a scheduled alarm would keep the note's text in the system's own storage and show it on your lock screen when it fired.

Locked notes are not copied to a new phone during a device transfer — the key cannot move with them. Export a backup first if you are changing devices.

Presentations, diaries and AI pictures

Presentations and diaries are built on your device. They leave it only when you share or save the file yourself. Reading a diary aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice.

AI pictures are optional and off by default. If you switch them on for a presentation, VoiceJot sends the titles of that presentation's slides — the headings you gave them, for example “Pricing” — and two of the theme's colours to VoiceJot's picture service, which returns an illustration for each. The exact titles are listed on screen before anything is sent. Nothing else from your notes is sent: not the text on the slides, not the presentation's own title, not any other note. Because a slide title is something you wrote, avoid putting anything in one that you would not want to send. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers AI, keeps no log of what it is asked to draw, stores nothing about you, and uses no account or identifier; like any web request, it necessarily sees your IP address, which it uses only to limit abuse. This is the only thing the app's internet access is used for.

“Sharpen it with an AI app” is different: it hands the outline to an app you choose through Android's share sheet. What that app does with it is governed by its own policy.

Microphone

VoiceJot requests the microphone permission only to turn your speech into text when you tap the mic button. Speech-to-text is performed by your device's built-in speech recognition service. VoiceJot does not record, store, or transmit audio itself.

Depending on your device and settings, the system speech-recognition service (for example, Google's) may process the audio to produce the transcription. That processing is governed by your device/OS provider's privacy policy, not VoiceJot's. VoiceJot prefers on-device recognition where your device supports it, and only ever receives the resulting text, only while you are actively using the mic.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used solely for voice-to-text, only while you hold a voice-note session. Never used in the background.
  • Internet (INTERNET): used only to fetch AI pictures for a presentation when you switch that on. Never used otherwise — no analytics, no ads, no sync.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show a reminder you set yourself. Requested the first time you set one.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Android clears pending alarms when the phone restarts, so VoiceJot re-registers your existing reminders after a reboot. Nothing else runs at startup.
  • Alarms & reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): lets a reminder fire at the minute you asked for rather than whenever the system next batches alarms. Optional — you grant it in system settings, and reminders still work without it, just approximately.

Children

VoiceJot does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
