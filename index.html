VoiceJot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

VoiceJot is designed to be private by default.

Summary: VoiceJot has no account, no ads and no analytics, and your notes — and any photos in them — stay on your phone. The one exception is optional and off by default: if you turn on AI pictures for a presentation, the titles of its slides are sent to draw them.

What we collect

Nothing. VoiceJot has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect or sell any personal information. The only thing VoiceJot itself ever sends anywhere is described under AI pictures below, and only when you switch them on.

Your notes

Your notes are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. VoiceJot never sends your notes anywhere, and no VoiceJot server stores anything about you. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Android offers apps an automatic backup to the user's own Google Drive. VoiceJot excludes your notes from it, so they are not copied to any cloud service, including Google's. On Android 12 and newer they are still included when you transfer directly from an old phone to a new one, because that copies straight between the two devices; on older versions of Android the two cannot be separated, so notes are left out of that transfer as well. Either way, Settings › Backup exports everything as a file you control.

Photos

If you add photos to notes, you pick each one with Android's photo picker, which hands VoiceJot that one photo — the app has no access to the rest of your gallery and holds no storage permission. VoiceJot keeps its own copy in its private storage, turned upright, scaled down to at most 1600 pixels, and saved without the camera's metadata, so the copy carries no location, date or device details. The copies are treated exactly like your notes: they stay on your device, are excluded from cloud backup, and go with your notes in a direct phone-to-phone transfer. A backup file lists photos by name only and doesn't contain them. Apart from that transfer, a photo leaves the phone only inside a diary PDF that you share or save yourself. Photos can't be added to a locked folder, because they are not encrypted.

Trips

A diary can have trip dates — when it started and when you are due home. They are stored on your device with the folder. If you place the trip countdown widget, it shows the days left and the folder's name on your home screen; a locked folder is never shown there.

Presentations, diaries and AI pictures

Presentations and diaries are built on your device and leave it only when you share or save the file yourself; a diary PDF includes the photos in its notes. A text report is handed to the app you pick in the share sheet, in the same way. Reading a diary aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice.

AI pictures are optional and off by default. If you switch them on for a presentation, VoiceJot sends the titles of that presentation's slides — the headings you gave them, for example “Pricing” — and two of the theme's colours to VoiceJot's picture service, which returns an illustration for each. The exact titles are listed on screen before anything is sent. Nothing else from your notes is sent: not the text on the slides, not the presentation's own title, not any other note. Because a slide title is something you wrote, avoid putting anything in one that you would not want to send. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers AI, keeps no log of what it is asked to draw, stores nothing about you and uses no account or identifier; like any web request it sees your IP address, which it uses only to limit abuse. This is the only thing the app's internet access is used for.

“Sharpen it with an AI app” is different: it hands the outline to an app you choose through Android's share sheet. What that app does with it is governed by its own policy.

Back-in-signal reminder

If you write notes while your phone has no connection, VoiceJot asks Android to let it know when a connection returns, and may then show one notification offering to send what you wrote. To do this it only asks Android whether a connection exists; it does not connect to anything, and it never sends anything by itself — tapping the reminder lets you choose what to send and with which app. It is on by default, appears at most once every 12 hours, never names a locked folder, and can be turned off in Settings › Reminders.

Purchases

VoiceJot Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; VoiceJot only learns from Google Play whether Pro is unlocked on your account, and never sees your payment details. The purchase is covered by Google Play's own terms and privacy policy.

Ratings

After you have shared or saved a presentation or diary, VoiceJot may ask Google Play to show its rating sheet — at most once every four months. The sheet is Google Play's own: any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play under Google's privacy policy, and VoiceJot is not told whether you left one.

Locked folders

A folder can be locked. Its notes are then encrypted on the device with a key held in the Android Keystore and released only after you confirm your fingerprint, PIN, pattern or password. The key never leaves the device's secure hardware, and VoiceJot keeps the decrypted notes in memory only while the folder is open — closing the app locks it again.

Locked folders need Android 11 or newer. On older versions the system cannot tie an encryption key to your PIN or pattern — only to a fingerprint — which would mean losing every note in the folder the moment a new fingerprint was added to the phone. Rather than ship that, the feature is simply absent there.

Two things this does not cover, stated plainly: the folder's name is stored unencrypted so the app can show that it exists while locked, so avoid putting anything revealing in the name; and a locked note cannot carry a reminder, because a scheduled alarm would keep the note's text in the system's own storage and show it on your lock screen when it fired. For the same reason the daily diary reminder pauses while its folder is locked.

Locked notes are not copied to a new phone during a device transfer — the key cannot move with them. Export a backup first if you are changing devices.

Microphone

VoiceJot requests the microphone permission only to turn your speech into text when you tap the mic button. Speech-to-text is performed by your device's built-in speech recognition service. VoiceJot does not record, store, or transmit audio itself.

Depending on your device and settings, the system speech-recognition service (for example, Google's) may process the audio to produce the transcription. That processing is governed by your device/OS provider's privacy policy, not VoiceJot's. VoiceJot prefers on-device recognition where your device supports it, and only ever receives the resulting text, only while you are actively using the mic.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used solely for voice-to-text, only while you hold a voice-note session. Never used in the background.
  • Internet (INTERNET): used only to fetch AI pictures for a presentation when you switch that on. Never used otherwise — no analytics, no ads, no sync.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used only to ask Android whether the phone has a connection, for the back-in-signal reminder. It does not let the app connect to anything.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show reminders — a note's reminder (one you set, or one taken from a follow-up's date, which you can undo), the daily diary reminder if you turn it on, and the back-in-signal reminder unless you turn it off. Requested the first time a reminder is set.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Android clears pending alarms when the phone restarts, so VoiceJot re-registers your existing reminders after a reboot, and Android keeps the back-in-signal reminder's wait for a connection. Nothing else runs at startup.
  • Photos: none. The photo picker hands over only the photos you pick, so no storage or media permission is requested.
  • Alarms & reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): lets a reminder fire at the minute you asked for rather than whenever the system next batches alarms. Optional — you grant it in system settings, and reminders still work without it, just approximately.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the app ask Google Play for the one-time Pro purchase.

Children

VoiceJot does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
